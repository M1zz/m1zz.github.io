Botaejwo

Collect everyone's photos with one QR code, as originals, right where they were taken in your Photos app.

What it does

No more waiting days for people to send their photos after an event. The host shows a QR code, and guests scan it with their camera and send their photos on the spot.

How it works

  • The host creates an event in Botaejwo and shows its QR code.
  • Guests scan it with the iPhone camera. There's no app to install. An App Clip opens for a moment.
  • Guests pick the photos and videos they want to send.
  • Everything goes straight into the host's Photos app, in an album named after the event.

Why it's different

  • Nothing to install. Guests send through an App Clip. No account, no sign in.
  • Originals, untouched. HEIC photos, Live Photos and videos arrive without being recompressed.
  • Right where they belong. Received photos sit at the date they were taken, not at today, so they blend in with your own shots.
  • No server holding your photos. On the same Wi-Fi, photos go straight from the guest's iPhone to the host's.
  • See who sent what. When guests add their name, it appears with each of their photos.

Good to know

  • Photos arrive while the host keeps the event screen open.
  • If a photo can't be sent directly over Wi-Fi, it's encrypted and passes briefly through iCloud, then deleted as soon as the host receives it.
  • A new QR code is made each time the event screen opens. Older codes stop working.

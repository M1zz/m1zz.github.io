Privacy Policy

Botaejwo · Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Botaejwo has no accounts, and the developer runs no server that stores your photos. Photos travel from the sender's iPhone to the receiver's iPhone. The developer never keeps or views them.

1. Information we collect

Botaejwo does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or account information. There is no sign up or sign in.

The only information the app handles is listed below, and it stays on users' devices. Separately, the app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can tell whether it is working well (see section 4).

  • Photos and videos: only the items the sender chooses. Information inside the files, such as when and where they were taken, travels with them and is recorded as is in the receiver's Photos library.
  • Sender's name: optional. It is used only to show the receiver who sent each photo and is stored on the receiver's device. It is also saved on the sender's device so they don't have to type it again.
  • Event names and counts: stored only on the receiver's device.

2. How photos travel

  • On the same Wi-Fi: the sender's iPhone connects directly to the receiver's iPhone. No server sits in between.
  • When a direct connection isn't possible: the files and their details are encrypted on the device, placed briefly in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database, and then picked up by the receiver. The key to unlock them exists only in the QR code, so no one other than the person who scanned it and the receiver, including the developer, can see the contents. Each item is deleted as soon as it reaches the receiver's Photos library.
  • The developer does not operate any separate server.

3. Permissions on your device

  • Photos (receiver): needed to add received photos to the Photos app and create the event album.
  • Local Network: needed to connect to the other iPhone on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Senders don't need to grant photo access. The app uses Apple's photo picker, so only the items they choose are shared with it.

4. Sharing and outside services

  • We do not share personal information with third parties.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, third-party analytics tools or tracking technologies.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: to learn how many devices use the app and to fix problems, the app sends the following to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit): a random ID created at install (not linked to you, your Apple ID, your device or any other app; it changes if you reinstall), the app version, iOS version, region and language setting, how many times and on which days the app was opened, the install date, and crash or hang reports (device type and technical details). No name, contact details, location or photos are included, and only the developer reads it. It is sent only when you are signed in to iCloud.
  • When photos pass through iCloud, they use Apple's infrastructure and remain encrypted the whole time.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Received photos stay in the receiver's Photos library and can be deleted there at any time.
  • Event information is removed when the event is deleted in the app or the app is deleted.
  • Items that pass through iCloud are deleted as soon as the receiver picks them up.

6. Children's privacy

Botaejwo does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Something to keep in mind

Photos may contain the location where they were taken. If you'd rather not share it, turn off location under Options in the photo picker before sending.

8. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
